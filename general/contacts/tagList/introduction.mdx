## What is a <span style={{color:"#ca8a04"}}>Group List</span> ?

We essentially organize your contacts into groups to form a contact list. A contact list is just a group of users that we intend to target.

## How to add a <span style={{color:"#ca8a04"}}>Group List</span> ?

Follow these steps to add a group list:<br/>

Login into the [dashboard](https://login.bolifyai.com/admin/dashboard) and open [Group List](https://login.bolifyai.com/admin/tags) section.<br/>
Then:

<span style={{ fontWeight: 800 }}>Step 1 :</span> Cick on the first 'Groups' button.

<span style={{ fontWeight: 800 }}>Step 2 :</span> Add Name and description <br />

<span style={{ fontWeight: 800 }}>Step 3 :</span> Cick on 'Save' button
