
<Info>
    This page explains the steps you need to follow to set up the Monday
    connection in Bolify AI. If any of the steps are outdated, please let us know!
</Info>

1. Login to your Monday account: [https://monday.com](https://monday.com).
2. Click on the account logo and go to the **Developers** page.
3. Click on the **My access tokens** tab on the left.
4. Click on the **Show** button and copy the access token from the page to the `API Token` field on Bolify AI.
5. Now, you can start using the Monday connection with Bolify AI.
