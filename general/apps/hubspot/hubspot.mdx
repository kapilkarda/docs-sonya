
<Info>
    This page explains the steps you need to follow to set up the Hubspot connection in Bolify AI. If any of the steps are outdated, please let us know!
</Info>

1. Go to the [HubSpot Developer page](https://developers.hubspot.com/).
2. Login into your developer account.
3. Click on the **Manage apps** button.
4. Click on the **Create app** button.
5. Fill the **Public app name** field with the name of your API app.
6. Go to the **Auth** tab.
7. Fill the **Redirect URL(s)** field with the OAuth Redirect URL from the Bolify AI connection creation page.
8. Go to the **Scopes** tab.
9. Select the scopes you want to use with Bolify AI.
10. Click on the **Create App** button.
11. Go back to the **Auth** tab.
12. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** values.
13. Paste the **Client ID** value into Bolify AI as **Client ID**, respectively.
14. Paste the **Client Secret** value into Bolify AI as **Client Secret**, respectively.
15. Click the **Submit** button on Bolify AI.
16. Now, you can start using the HubSpot connection with Bolify AI.
